How it works

1

Say “Pepe”

Or press the big button. Pepe listens only after its name, and speaks slowly and clearly.

2

It remembers for them

Pills on time, the doctor tomorrow, a granddaughter’s birthday, what to buy. It asks “Did you take it?” and never suggests a double dose.

3

It keeps them company

It learns their stories and interests, follows up on them, and knows when to stay quiet. Tell it “talk less” and it does, from then on.

Our promises

Listens only after its name

The wake word is recognised on the device. Nothing leaves the home before it.

Nothing sent without a yes

Pepe asks before it messages anyone, except the emergency alert they choose at setup. They decide what the family sees.

Honest, always

It says it’s an assistant, admits mistakes, and never pressures or sells.

Not an alarm system

KORE adds to teleasistencia; it never replaces it. In an emergency, their button and 112 come first.

For families

Stay in the loop without spying. If they agree, you can see that all is well, get an alert if medication is missed, and send messages and photos Pepe shares with them.

  • They choose what you can see, and can change it by voice
  • Conversations stay private: only agreed alerts are shared, and the person who looks after their KORE reads recent conversations only if they say yes, to fix problems
  • Their memory is kept on the speaker at home. What they say to Pepe is processed by our speech and AI providers to answer, and not kept by us
